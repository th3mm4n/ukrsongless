SoundCloud check

Developer tool. Open this file in a normal browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari; also try your phone) and press the button. For every song in songs.js with an sc link it loads the widget, plays the first second twice (cold, then buffered) and reports timing. You will hear each clip: also check by ear that it is the right song and that the start sounds right (set start to skip a silent intro). Headless Chrome in the Claude sandbox cannot play DRM streams, so this page is how the official (DRM) uploads get verified.
